SLIDE 1: OVERVIEW
─────────────────
- Purpose: Predict 30-day readmission risk for mental health patients
- Model: XGBoost + Random Forest + Logistic Regression ensemble
- Input: PHQ-9, GAD-7 scores + demographics + history
- Output: Risk score (0-10) + classification (Low/Medium/High)
